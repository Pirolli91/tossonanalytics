## 4. Section-by-Section Template Specification (Implemented in Deliverable 6 & Sample Issue #0)

1. **Masthead & Provenance Strip:** Newsletter title, Dr. Soneye's PhD credentials, issue number, target dispatch date, build timestamp, and clickable `tossonanalytics.com/insights/<slug>` web archive permalink.
2. **Standing Section 1 — ⚡ The Top Line:** Headline, monospace `Key Number` box, 3–4 sentence synthesis, and full-URL primary citations.
3. **Standing Section 2 — 📅 NC & Federal Deadlines, Hearings & Dockets:** Chronological table with `Date`, bracketed audience tag (`[NC Water Systems]`, `[NC Rulemaking]`, `[MDL Claimants]`, `[NC Utilities]`), `Docket/Action`, instructions, and full URL.
4. **Layer 1 — North Carolina Layer (`#14b8a6` Teal — First, Longest, 4–6 Items):** Covers NC DEQ, EMC dockets (`15A NCAC 02B .0512`, `02L .0202`, `18C .1540`), Chemours consent-order expansions, municipal water authorities (CFPUA, Raleigh, Cary, Pittsboro), NC General Assembly bills, and NC Collaboratory / university studies. Every item contains:
   - Declarative action headline
   - NC metadata pill strip (`NC Scope / Counties`, `Docket/Rule`, `Compounds`, `Screening Benchmark`)
   - 2–4 sentence original summary (never copied text)
   - Highlighted `Why It Matters` operational takeaway
   - Full clickable URL citation (`source_id`, `source_name`, `https://...`)
5. **Standing Section 3 — 🚰 North Carolina Private Wells Corner:** Displays the verified `2.4M–3.3M` population methodology note inline, free Chemours/Parsons sampling eligibility (`(910) 678-1100` across 10 counties), statewide NC DEQ PFAS Alternate Water Program (`$3,500` Bernard Allen Fund reimbursement / free RO), and certified EPA Method `533`/`537.1` lab guidance.
6. **Standing Section 4 — 📊 Tosson Data Corner:** One metric/chart explanation from Tosson's NC UCMR 5 tracker ([`https://tossonanalytics.com/dashboard`](https://tossonanalytics.com/dashboard)), strictly using §1.3 screening language.
7. **Layer 2 — United States Layer (`#38bdf8` Sky Blue — Second, 2–4 Items):** EPA rulemaking, Federal Register actions, D.C. Circuit / AFFF MDL litigation, ATSDR/NIEHS guidance, and comparative state primacy actions.
8. **Layer 3 — Global Layer (`#fbbf24` Amber — Last & Shortest, 1–2 Items):** ECHA REACH universal PFAS restriction, EU PFHxA/AFFF phase-outs, and OECD lifecycle reports with clear NC/US relevance.
9. **Standing Section 5 — 🔬 Research Radar (`#a855f7` Purple — 3 Papers):** Exact title, authors, journal, year, DOI, PMID, full URL, plain-language summary, and an explicit **Scope & Claim Boundary (§1.3)** note.
10. **Standing Section 6 — 🔭 What to Watch Next Week:** 3–4 forward-looking bullets.

## 5. Cadence & Weekly Production Calendar

- **Recommended Send Day & Time:** **Monday Mornings at 07:30 ET.**
  - *Why Monday 07:30 ET:* (1) Honors the promise already published on [`/regulatory-watch`](https://tossonanalytics.com/regulatory-watch) (*"delivered every Monday morning"*); (2) Positions the briefing at the top of NC utility managers', DEQ regulators', and advocates' inboxes as they set their weekly agenda; and (3) Fits cleanly into Muse's existing Wednesday data refresh + Friday policy scan schedule without weekend crunch.
- **Weekly Production Slot (Total Compute $\le 15$ Minutes):**
  - **Wednesday ~06:09 ET (Existing Muse Job):** Muse runs the hardened UCMR 5 / WQP and NC news refresh.
  - **Friday ~16:00 ET (Automated Draft Build — ~5 mins compute):** Muse runs `node newsletter/bin/run-pipeline.js`, which ingests the week's verified registry sources, enforces all §1.1–§1.5 guardrail gates, compiles the review package (`HTML` + `MDX` + `TXT` + `review-package.json`), and stages a **Draft** (`status: "draft"`) in Buttondown.
  - **Weekend / Sunday Evening (Owner Approval Gate — §1.5):** Dr. Soneye reviews the staged review package, requests any edits, and gives explicit send approval.
  - **Monday 07:30 ET (Approved Dispatch & Archive Deploy):** Upon Dr. Soneye's explicit approval, Muse triggers the Buttondown dispatch and pushes the approved `.mdx` issue to `master` so Cloudflare Pages deploys the permanent web archive URL simultaneously.
