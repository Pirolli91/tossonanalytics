### Site 3 — `https://pfastoxdatabase.org/` (PFAS-Tox Database)
- **What It Is & Architecture:** A systematic evidence map created by Katie Pelch, Anna Reade, Carol Kwiatkowski, and Julia Varshavsky (managed at [Northeastern SSEHRI](https://cssh.northeastern.edu/ssehri/pfas-tox-database/) / [PFAS Project Lab](https://pfasproject.com/pfas-toxic-database/)). The site embeds a 5-sheet Tableau Public workbook (`Home Page`, `PFAS-Tox Database`, `Chemical Structures`, `Health Outcome Glossary`, `About`) mapping **1,067 peer-reviewed studies** (`505` human, `385` animal, `220` in vitro) across **29 emerging/replacement PFAS** (excluding PFOA/PFOS; includes NC-critical fluoroethers **GenX/HFPO-DA**, **Nafion BP-2**, **Hydro-EVE**, and **ADONA**) and **15 health outcome categories** ([PFAS-Tox Database](https://pfastoxdatabase.org/), [PubMed 35908389](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35908389/)).
- **Curation & Tooling Model:** Uses PECO systematic review inclusion criteria; includes both statistically significant and non-significant findings to prevent publication-bias distortion; and allows filtering by study type, early-life exposure, and **financial conflict of interest (COI)**.
- **Newsletter Practice:** Zero email capture on `pfastoxdatabase.org`; companion [PFAS Project Lab Newsletters](https://pfasproject.com/newsletters/) publishes monthly.
- **Update Cadence & Fragility:** Frozen since 2021–2022 (`Literature Search Last Updated: January 25, 2021`; `Database Data Last Updated: May 11, 2021`; `Database Display Last Updated: August 9, 2022` on [PFAS-Tox Database](https://pfastoxdatabase.org/)), and the embedded Tableau Server iframe throws `"Unexpected Error"` banners on mobile/automated user agents.
- **Licensing Posture:** Underlying paper (Pelch et al., *Environment International* 167, 2022, 107408) is licensed `CC BY-NC-ND 4.0` ([Full Paper PDF](https://knowledge.lancashire.ac.uk/id/eprint/43469/1/1-s2.0-S016041202200335X-main.pdf)) with supplemental files at [https://osf.io/f9upx/](https://osf.io/f9upx/) $\rightarrow$ **`USE_BY_CITING`**.
- **Borrow / Adapt / Avoid for Tosson (Including What to Do with `public/data/pfas-tox-db.json`):**
  - **Borrow:** Three-part provenance timestamps (*Literature Cutoff*, *Data Ingest*, *Display Update*), COI tagging, and strict refusal to overclaim beyond study design (human vs. animal vs. in vitro).
  - **Adapt (Action for Tosson's 260-byte `pfas-tox-db.json` stub):** Do **not** embed PFAS-Tox's desktop-only Tableau iframe or attempt to mirror 1,000+ general studies. Instead, replace the 260-byte `pfas-tox-db.json` stub on [`/toxicology`](https://tossonanalytics.com/toxicology) with a focused, native JSON **NC Priority PFAS & Fluoroether Toxicology Matrix** (covering the 6 EPA-regulated PFAS plus Cape Fear fluoroethers GenX, Nafion BP-2, PMPA, PEPA, PFO5DoA, Hydro-EVE, PFMOAA, and TFA) that links directly out to [https://pfastoxdatabase.org/](https://pfastoxdatabase.org/) for pre-2021 evidence maps while curating 2022–2026 NC studies via PubMed/OpenAlex.
  - **Avoid:** Calling a static dataset a `"living database"` when ingestion has stopped.

### Site 4 — `https://pfascentral.org/` (PFAS Central — Closest Curation Analogue)
- **What It Is & Architecture:** Operated by the [Green Science Policy Institute](https://greensciencepolicy.org/our-work/pfas/) and Northeastern SSEHRI ([About Us](https://pfascentral.org/about/)). Features a 3-card `Hot Items` top row (`News`, `Science`, `Policy`) above chronological feeds for [News](https://pfascentral.org/news/), [Science](https://pfascentral.org/science/), [Policy](https://pfascentral.org/policy/), and `Opportunities`, plus [PFAS Basics](https://pfascentral.org/pfas-basics/), [Resources](https://pfascentral.org/resources/) (filterable by 7 audiences: `Public`, `Policymaker`, `Business`, `Scientist`, `Medical`, `Journalist`, `NGO`), [PFAS-Free Products](https://pfascentral.org/pfas-free-products/), and the 60+ database [Data Hub](https://pfascentral.org/data-hub/).
- **Curation Standards & Cadence:** Updated multiple times per week (e.g., Nov 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9, 2026 on [PFAS Central](https://pfascentral.org/)). Every curated paper in [Science](https://pfascentral.org/science/) gets a 1-to-2 sentence plain-English translation beneath the citation. In the [Data Hub](https://pfascentral.org/data-hub/), every external database entry carries an actionable `"TIP:"` or `"Caveats:"` instruction explaining exact search filters (e.g., selecting `"Well"` under `"Site Type"` in the US Water Quality Data Portal, or filtering by PFAS NAICS codes in EPA ECHO). In [PFAS-Free Products](https://pfascentral.org/pfas-free-products/), GSPI enforces a written [Eligibility One-Pager PDF](https://pfascentral.org/perch/resources/products/pfas-free-products-list-eligibility-one-pager.pdf) requiring either an explicit online corporate policy audited quarterly or public Total Organic Fluorine (TOF) test data.
- **Newsletter Practice:** [https://pfascentral.org/newsletter/](https://pfascentral.org/newsletter/) signs users up for the **monthly** Green Science Policy Institute newsletter (covering all "Six Classes" of chemicals, not just PFAS) via a high-friction **10-field CRM form** requiring physical home/work street address.

Crucially, [Terms & Conditions (`https://pfascentral.org/terms-conditions/`)](https://pfascentral.org/terms-conditions/) prohibits reproducing, duplicating, copying, or commercially using any portion of information obtained from PFAS Central $\rightarrow$ **`USE_BY_CITING` (Excluded from automated feed scraping in `04-excluded-sources-log.md`)**.

### Site 5 — `https://pfas-exchange.org/` (The PFAS Exchange — Silent Spring Institute / PFAS-REACH)
- **What It Is & Architecture:** A multilingual (`English`, `Español`, `Português`) drinking-water and biomonitoring resource hub built under NIEHS Grant `R01ES028311` ([The PFAS Exchange](https://pfas-exchange.org/)). Organizes entry around **four audience pathways** ([Impacted Communities](https://pfas-exchange.org/impacted-communities/), [Clinicians](https://pfas-exchange.org/clinicians/), [Firefighters](https://pfas-exchange.org/firefighters/), [Water Utilities](https://pfas-exchange.org/water-utilities/)) and three tools: a faceted [Resource Hub](https://pfas-exchange.org/tools/resource-hub/), the [Contamination Map](https://pfas-exchange.org/tools/contamination-map/) (with a 5-tab [North Carolina State Report](https://www-pfas.pfas-exchange.org/report/map/?state=North%20Carolina)), and the interactive ["What's My Exposure?" Tool](https://pfas-exchange.org/tools/exposure-tool/).
- **How the Results-Interpretation Tool Works:** Documented on [ReportBack How-To](https://reportback.org/pfas-exchange/report/how-to-use-this-tool) and [Communities With High PFAS](https://reportback.org/pfas-exchange/report/communities-with-high-pfas), users enter drinking-water (`ppt`/`ng/L`/`ppb`) or blood (`ng/mL`) results. The tool provides annotated visual instructions for **adding linear + branched PFOS isomers** on Quest reports (`1.18 + 1.36 = 2.54 ng/mL`) and selecting the **Serum Equivalent** column on EmpowerDX finger-prick reports ([PFAS Blood Testing Guide](https://pfas-exchange.org/resource-hub/pfas-blood-testing-what-you-need-to-know/)). It plots water results against EPA MCLs/state guidelines and blood results on a base-10 log scale against **500 CDC NHANES participants** (medians: PFOS `4.3 ng/mL`, PFOA `1.5 ng/mL`, PFHxS `1.1 ng/mL`, PFNA `0.4 ng/mL`) and the **NASEM `20 ng/mL` clinical threshold**.
- **Well-Owner & Clinician Guides:** Its [Drinking Water Guide (updated July 28, 2026)](https://pfas-exchange.org/resource-hub/pfas-and-drinking-water-what-you-should-know/) instructs well owners how to find **NELAC/TNI-accredited labs** running **EPA Method 533 or 537.1** and verify filters under **NSF/ANSI 53** (carbon/ion exchange) or **NSF/ANSI 58** (RO) for `"Total PFAS Reduction"`. Its [Clinicians Portal](https://pfas-exchange.org/clinicians/) and [Blood Testing Guide](https://pfas-exchange.org/resource-hub/pfas-blood-testing-what-you-need-to-know/) provide CME videos, **ICD-10 code `Z13.88`**, **Quest code `39307`**, **CPT code `82542`**, kidney-disease albuminuria caveats, and legal chain-of-custody notes.
- **Newsletter Practice:** **Zero visible email capture form** on the redesigned site ([The PFAS Exchange](https://pfas-exchange.org/), [Contact](https://pfas-exchange.org/contact/)).
- **Licensing Posture:** `Silent Spring Institute © 2026 Silent Spring Institute` $\rightarrow$ **`USE_BY_CITING`**.
- **Borrow / Adapt / Avoid for Tosson:**
  - **Borrow:** 4-persona audience pathways, NELAC `EPA 533`/`537.1` and `NSF/ANSI 53`/`58` filter checklist, and clinician blood-testing codes (`ICD-10 Z13.88`, Quest `39307`, CPT `82542`).
  - **Adapt:** Build a lightweight static **NC Well-Water Screening Calculator** on `tossonanalytics.com` (and feature its logic in the newsletter's Private Wells Corner) that compares a resident's lab report against (a) the 10.0 ppt Chemours Consent Order filtration threshold, (b) the NC DEQ PFAS Alternate Water Program ($3,500 Bernard Allen Fund) eligibility criteria, and (c) EPA's 2024 MCL benchmarks (`4.0 ppt` PFOA/PFOS, `10.0 ppt` GenX/PFNA/PFHxS).
  - **Avoid:** Launching interactive tools without an email signup capture, or leaving NC state map contamination entries frozen at 2013–2018 ([North Carolina Map Report](https://www-pfas.pfas-exchange.org/report/map/?state=North%20Carolina)).
