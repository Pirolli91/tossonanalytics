## 1. Executive Summary of What Is Strong vs. What Must Change Now

### What Is Strong (Preserve & Build Upon)
1. **Authoritative NC-Specific Trackers:** Both [`/dashboard`](https://tossonanalytics.com/dashboard) (`70 counties · 47 above MCL benchmark · 177 water systems · Highest: Nash (122.5× benchmark) · MCL: 4 ppt PFOA/PFOS`) and [`/policy-tracker`](https://tossonanalytics.com/policy-tracker) (`$590M` NC Chemours settlement, Corteva/Vylor guarantor litigation, `15A NCAC 02B .0512` / `02H .0923`, `15A NCAC 18C .1540`, `HB 569`, `HB 881`, `2026 State Budget PFAS Package`) deliver higher regulatory precision for North Carolina than any of the seven academic/NGO reference sites.
2. **Clear Screening Framing on Primary Trackers:** Both [`/`](https://tossonanalytics.com/) (*"Severity scores benchmark the highest UCMR 5 result against the 4.0 ppt EPA MCLs — a screening comparison, not a compliance finding"*) and [`/regulatory-watch`](https://tossonanalytics.com/regulatory-watch) (*"UCMR 5 measures occurrence, not compliance: a detection — or a single result above an MCL benchmark — is a screening signal, never a violation finding"*) model the exact factual discipline required by §1.3.
3. **Strong Editorial Voice in `/insights`:** The three flagship September 25, 2026 essays ([`2026-09-25-epa-mcl-compliance-countdown`](https://tossonanalytics.com/insights/2026-09-25-epa-mcl-compliance-countdown), [`2026-09-25-nash-county-ucmr5-data-refresh`](https://tossonanalytics.com/insights/2026-09-25-nash-county-ucmr5-data-refresh), and [`2026-09-25-private-well-testing-gap`](https://tossonanalytics.com/insights/2026-09-25-private-well-testing-gap)) read like a seasoned PhD engineer speaking directly to utility directors and advocates.

### Critical Defects Uncovered Across the Live Site (Summary)
Our audit uncovered **7 P0 (Fix Immediately)** issues—including a silent-loss subscribe form, broken homepage stat counters rendering `10 NC Counties`, `3 PFAS Compounds`, and `0 ppt EPA MCL`, a publication list discrepancy between [`/about`](https://tossonanalytics.com/about) and [`/capability-statement.pdf`](https://tossonanalytics.com/capability-statement.pdf), public `"Gemma 4"` AI attribution and raw LLM prompt leakage on [`/news`](https://tossonanalytics.com/news), and a factual error in the private-wells article telling well owners that county health departments and the NC PFAS Testing Network are the starting points for PFAS well testing—followed by **6 P1 (High-Priority)** and **5 P2 (Medium-Priority)** improvements.
